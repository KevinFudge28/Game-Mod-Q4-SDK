#Dungeon and Dragons Mod for Quake 4#

This is a simple Mod for Quake 4 that is based on a the game called Dungeon and Dragons. This mod will add fantasy-inspired RPG combat to the game. Instead of guns the player will have melee weapons and they can cast different kinds of spells that either deal damage or support the player in some way. There will also be 3 classes to choose from. Along with a level up system to unlock new weapons or spells. There will different kinds of DND items that will randomly spawn items that the player can interact with.
